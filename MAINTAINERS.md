# Maintainers

## Current maintainers

The project is currently stewarded by the founding maintainer:

- [@Vicky-RDP](https://github.com/Vicky-RDP) — project steward and repository administrator

The goal is to grow a diverse maintainer group across hardware, verification, software, compiler, documentation, and community work. Maintainers are selected for contribution quality and collaboration, not geography or employment.

## Contact and escalation

- General project questions: open a Discussion or Issue.
- Technical decisions: open a design discussion and tag the relevant workstream.
- Security issues: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public Issue.
- Code of Conduct concerns: contact the maintainers privately through the GitHub profile.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers keep review standards clear, protect the project’s open nature, document important decisions, welcome contributors, and avoid allowing one person or organization to become an unnecessary bottleneck.
