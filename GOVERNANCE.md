# Governance

India AI Inference Chip v1.0 is an open project with a transparent, merit-oriented decision process.

## Roles

- **Contributors** submit code, documentation, benchmarks, reviews, issues, and proposals.
- **Maintainers** review contributions, keep the project healthy, and make day-to-day decisions.
- **Workstream leads** coordinate a technical area and report status publicly.
- **Stewards** are trusted maintainers responsible for long-term project direction and releases.

Roles are earned through sustained, constructive contribution. Nationality, employer, seniority, and affiliation are not prerequisites.

## How decisions are made

1. Start with a public Issue, Discussion, or design document.
2. State the problem, alternatives, measurable success criteria, and risks.
3. Invite review from affected workstreams.
4. Record the decision and rationale in `docs/decisions/` for architectural changes.
5. Prefer rough consensus and working code; when consensus is not possible, maintainers document the decision and dissenting concerns.

Changes affecting public interfaces, numeric behavior, licensing, security, or fabrication require extra review and should not be merged as drive-by changes.

## Maintainer decisions

Maintainers may merge reviewed pull requests, triage issues, create releases, and revert changes that break the project. Maintainer decisions must be explainable from public project goals and evidence. A maintainer should recuse themselves from decisions involving a direct conflict of interest.

## Becoming a maintainer

Any contributor may be nominated after demonstrating reliable technical judgment, respectful collaboration, and sustained participation. Existing maintainers discuss nominations publicly and record the outcome in the repository.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes require a public proposal, at least two maintainer approvals, and a documented migration plan where existing contributors are affected.
