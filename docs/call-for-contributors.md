# Join the first India Inference Chip tapeout

We are building an open INT8 inference chip from India with contributors
everywhere. The first tapeout target is 23 July 2027, and the project is ready
for people who want to learn, review, prototype, document, or lead a workstream.

You can help with:

- SystemVerilog RTL and verification;
- FPGA prototyping, synthesis, physical design, and open-ASIC research;
- RISC-V firmware, drivers, compiler, quantization, and runtime;
- benchmark design for vision, audio, robotics, and edge workloads; or
- documentation, translations, community moderation, and release engineering.

Start here:

1. Read GETTING_STARTED.md.
2. Read docs/project-blueprint.md and docs/tapeout-roadmap.md.
3. Comment below with your time zone, the workstream you want to join, and one
   first task you can own.
4. Open a small issue-linked pull request, or use the contributor issue form.

You do not need a famous employer, a specific nationality, or prior tapeout
experience. We do need respectful collaboration, reproducible evidence, and
the discipline to keep the first chip small enough to finish.
